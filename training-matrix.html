DECMIL quick setup

One-click defaults so alerts & compliance switch on

Your spreadsheet doesn't store expiry periods or role requirements, so imported records read as "valid" until you set these. Apply sensible starting defaults, then fine-tune per course / role.

Validity rules (editable per course): First Aid 36mo · WAH/EWP/Forklift/Crane VOCs 24mo · High-risk work & plant licences 60mo · Inductions / Drug&Alcohol / Isolation / Light Vehicle 12mo · plant Operate & Test&Move VOCs 24mo. Trade qualifications stay non-expiring. Baseline requirements mark the universal site items (Induction, Drug&Alcohol, Isolation, Light Vehicle) as required for every role.

Spreadsheet import / export

Export the full register (one row per person & course) as CSV, or a wide grid matching a spreadsheet layout. Import people or training records from a CSV.

People CSV columns: name, role, employeeId, department.
Records CSV columns: employeeId (or name), courseCode (or courseName), completedDate (YYYY-MM-DD), evidenceUrl, notes.
